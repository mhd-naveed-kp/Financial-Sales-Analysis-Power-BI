# Financial Performance Dashboard – Power BI

## Project Overview

An interactive Financial Performance Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. This dashboard provides a detailed analysis of sales and profitability, helping to identify top-performing products, countries, business segments, and discount categories.

The report is organized into three pages: Home, Sales, and Profit, allowing users to explore different aspects of business performance.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Home Dashboard

Provides an overall summary of financial performance through key performance indicators and visualizations.

**Key Performance Indicators:**
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Units Sold
- Total Manufacturing Cost

**Visualizations:**
- Sales and Profit by Month
- Sales and Manufacturing Cost by Product
- Profit by Product
- Sales by Discount Band

**Key Insights:**
- Paseo is the leading product in terms of both sales and profit.
- Sales and profit performance varies across products.
- Discount categories show different contributions to overall sales and profitability.

### 2. Sales Analysis

Focuses on understanding sales performance across different business dimensions.

**Visualizations:**
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Product
- Sales by Business Segment
- Sales by Country (Map)
- Sales by Discount Band

**Key Insights:**
- The United States of America generates the highest overall sales among the five countries.
- Government is the largest contributor to total sales among business segments.
- Paseo contributes the highest product sales.
- Medium discount band accounts for the largest share of sales among discount categories.

### 3. Profit Analysis

Provides a detailed view of profitability across products, countries, segments, and discount categories.

**Visualizations:**
- Total Profit KPI
- Profit by Product
- Profit by Country (Map)
- Profit by Business Segment
- Monthly Profit Trend
- Profit by Discount Band

**Key Insights:**
- Paseo generates the highest profit among all products.
- France records the highest total profit among the five countries.
- Government generates the highest profit among business segments.
- Enterprise records a negative overall profit, indicating a loss.
- Low discount band generates the highest total profit compared with medium and high discount categories.

## Overall Business Insights

- Paseo is the strongest-performing product in both sales and profitability.
- Government is the leading business segment in terms of sales and profit.
- The United States leads in sales, while France leads in profitability.
- Low discount transactions contribute the highest total profit.
- Enterprise requires further investigation due to its negative profitability.

## Interactive Features

- Country, Product, and Segment slicers.
- Page navigation buttons.
- Bookmark functionality.
- Reset button to restore the report view.
- Interactive charts and cross-filtering.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform financial data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations. It helps users understand sales performance, identify profitable products and markets, and evaluate business segment performance.
